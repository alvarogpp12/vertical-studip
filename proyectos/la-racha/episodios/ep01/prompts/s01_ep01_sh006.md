Night, inside. The room is empty of people.

CONSTRAINTS (inmutable)
Photoreal live-action. Identities, hair and wardrobe match their reference descriptions in every shot. Faces blink and breathe; eyes wet and alive with catch-lights. No music — diegetic sound only. No spoken lines in this piece. Location descriptions control geometry, materials and light only; nothing added beyond what is described. No readable text anywhere in frame. No logos. Background people stay out of focus and unidentifiable. Subject kept inside the vertical safe zone: eyes on the upper third, lower fifth clear for subtitles. Avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs, avoid temporal flicker, avoid identity drift.

POSITIVE LOCKS

Readable text on any screen = failed take.
A brand logo in frame = failed take.
