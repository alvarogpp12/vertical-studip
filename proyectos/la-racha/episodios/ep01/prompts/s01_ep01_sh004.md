# STYLE PREFIX (inmutable durante toda la serie)
Style: sober documentary realism, vertical 9:16 composition, natural low light, palette #1B2430 #C9A227 #D0342C, fine 35mm grain, 50mm lens, tripod, slow tilts and push-ins only.

SCENE CONTEXT
Dusk. Ground level, very close to the pavement.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

A narrow street in a Spanish provincial city at dusk: four-storey cream facades with green louvred shutters and wrought-iron balconies, a tiled bar front with a dark green awning, warm sodium streetlights, wet pavement, parked scooters against the kerb.

LOCATION MAP
Nil baja por la acera de la izquierda, la fachada del bar a su derecha, la calle se hunde al fondo. La cámara mira calle abajo.

FIRST FRAME
Two worn trainers mid-step on wet stone, the kerb and a scooter wheel behind them.

SHOTS
Shot 1: the trainers take one more step and stop, weight settling onto the left foot.
Closing: they stay still; the right heel lifts a centimetre and comes back down.

DIALOGUE
None. Nobody speaks in this shot.

PERFORMANCE
The camera stays at ankle height for the whole shot. All the information is in the feet stopping.

CAMERA
Static, locked-off, low angle at ankle height.

LIGHTING
Sodium light from above-left, long reflection on the wet stone.

PHYSICS
Water in the seam of the pavement shivers when the foot lands.

AUDIO
No music — diegetic sound only. One footstep, then room tone and distant traffic.

CONSTRAINTS (inmutable)
Photoreal live-action. Identities, hair and wardrobe match their reference descriptions in every shot. Faces blink and breathe; eyes wet and alive with catch-lights. No music — diegetic sound only. No spoken lines in this piece. Location descriptions control geometry, materials and light only; nothing added beyond what is described. No readable text anywhere in frame. No logos. Background people stay out of focus and unidentifiable. Subject kept inside the vertical safe zone: eyes on the upper third, lower fifth clear for subtitles.
