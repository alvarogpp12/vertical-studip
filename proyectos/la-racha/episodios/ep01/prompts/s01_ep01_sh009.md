# STYLE PREFIX (inmutable durante toda la serie)
Style: sober documentary realism, vertical 9:16 composition, natural low light, palette #1B2430 #C9A227 #D0342C, fine 35mm grain, 50mm lens, tripod, slow tilts and push-ins only.

SCENE CONTEXT
Night, inside. His phone is face up on the ledge of the terminal.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

LOCATION MAP
Nil de pie frente al terminal del fondo, el mostrador a su izquierda de cámara, la fila de máquinas a su derecha. La cámara mira al fondo.

FIRST FRAME
His forearm and the lit phone on the ledge, his face out of the top of frame.

SHOTS
Shot 1: the phone lights up and buzzes twice against the ledge.
Shot 2: his hand comes down flat and covers it without stopping anywhere else.
Closing: the hand lifts away; the ledge is dark again.

DIALOGUE
None. Nobody speaks in this shot.

PERFORMANCE
The hand moves once, flat and direct, and goes straight back. It never picks the phone up. No fingers operating anything in close-up.

CAMERA
Slow tilt up from the ledge to his chin, tripod. One move only.

LIGHTING
Red and green from the screen; the phone adds a small cold white patch that disappears.

PHYSICS
The phone vibrates against the plastic ledge and shifts two centimetres.

AUDIO
No music — diegetic sound only. Two buzzes on plastic, machine hum.

CONSTRAINTS (inmutable)
Photoreal live-action. Identities, hair and wardrobe match their reference descriptions in every shot. Faces blink and breathe; eyes wet and alive with catch-lights. No music — diegetic sound only. No spoken lines in this piece. Location descriptions control geometry, materials and light only; nothing added beyond what is described. No readable text anywhere in frame. No logos. Background people stay out of focus and unidentifiable. Subject kept inside the vertical safe zone: eyes on the upper third, lower fifth clear for subtitles. Avoid jitter, avoid bent limbs, avoid temporal flicker, avoid identity drift.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Readable text on the phone = failed take.
Fingers in close-up = failed take.
Him picking the phone up = failed take.
